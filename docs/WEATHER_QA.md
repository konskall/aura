# Weather QA v10.3.1

Updated: 2026-10-20 (Europe/Athens)

## v10.3.1 premium UI verification

- Thirteen distinct native 480×480 captures cover sunny, cloudy, rainy, stormy, snowy, night, scrolled forecast, loading, no-city, offline, error, city entry and search results.
- Real SDL → LVGL pointer coverage passes **109/109 at 1×** and **109/109 at 2×**. The added checks prove vertical forecast scrolling, hourly-card detail selection and horizontal hourly swiping, in addition to the v10.3 routes.
- Clear, cloudy, rain, storm, snow and night each produced eight materially distinct frames over 2,450 ms: **48/48 frames**. The final full-suite weather cadence was 27.92–28.97 Hz; average LVGL handler time was 0.362–0.490 ms, maximum 3.336–4.653 ms; allocator endpoint use was 49,032–52,720 bytes at 1% fragmentation.
- The scrolled forecast capture was visually inspected after enforcing LVGL clip regions; forecast content no longer paints over the fixed header. The night crescent, rain/snow particles, layered clouds and storm treatment were also inspected.
- Open-Meteo parsing and cache tests retain exact WMO code plus hourly/current/daily precipitation probabilities. The 64 px IBM Plex numeric weather subset is generated by the pinned font pipeline and queried at runtime for all permitted glyphs.
- A post-change live UTF-8 `Αθήνα` request reached Open-Meteo with the new precipitation query, rendered a real night result and exited successfully. Its isolated capture is `artifacts/v10_3_1/live/weather-live-athens.bmp`.

- Eight distinct 480×480 states: ready, loading, no city, offline, error, city entry, search results and Quick Actions entry point.

- Real SDL → LVGL pointer tests pass **106/106 at 1×** and **106/106 at 2×**. Coverage includes the Weather tile, typed and preset search, result confirmation, refresh, city pill, Back/Home, Offline/Error retry, Settings route and assistant weather/change-city intents.

- Unit coverage validates coordinates/data bounds, WMO mapping, Greek intents, cached-offline behavior, valid/malformed geocoding and forecast JSON, and UTF-8 persistence round trips.
